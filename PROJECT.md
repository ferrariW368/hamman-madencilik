## 1. Mission



Build a fast, credible, visually distinctive international B2B website for HAMMARBLE that presents its natural stones, quarry sources, proven references and direct contact paths without turning the company into a generic luxury, SaaS or AI-generated brand.



The website must work in two contexts:

- Showcase: large-screen presentation, trade fairs and first impressions.

- Field utility: fast access from a phone to a stone, technical information, quarry location and contact.



## 2. Product Principles — LOCKED



1. Stone is the product. Design supports it.

2. Real quarry and stone imagery is the visual foundation.

3. AI is a production tool, never the visual identity.

4. Scroll moves through the website; it must not be required to operate the website.

5. Motion may create atmosphere but must never block navigation or information.

6. Mobile is a utility surface, not a reduced desktop showreel.

7. No company fact, technical value, project scope or location may be invented.

8. A complete functional V1 is more valuable than unfinished visual experiments.



## 3. Brand Direction — LOCKED



Direction: Raw Premium / Industrial Elegance / Quarry First.



The site should feel sober, physical, confident and high-quality. Use large real imagery, raw stone, quarry scale, machinery where relevant, close-up veins, disciplined typography and generous whitespace.



Avoid:

- generic luxury-hotel styling

- generic AI website aesthetics

- startup/SaaS visual language

- gratuitous gradients or glowing borders

- glassmorphism

- excessive card grids

- constant animation

- unsupported superlatives such as "world-class", "global leader" or "best"



There is no approved slogan. Do not invent one.



## 4. Primary Users — LOCKED



Primary users are international B2B natural-stone buyers, importers, distributors, architects/specifiers and existing contacts who may open the website during a trade fair or business conversation.



The site must make these actions fast:

- find a stone

- inspect its surface and block appearance

- access verified technical information/documents

- identify its quarry/origin

- navigate to a quarry where applicable

- see credible project/event proof

- contact the company directly

## 8. Stones — LOCKED



The site is block-sales oriented. Do not present the company as a slab/tile ecommerce business.



Home/collection uses a Collection + Focus model:

- all active stones remain visible

- Spider receives greater visual weight without "bestseller", "flagship" or "premium" badges

- mobile uses visible names and direct tap targets

- scrolling must not be used to select products



Real stone photography is mandatory for final production.



AI must never invent or alter a stone pattern in a way that represents it as a real HAMMARBLE material.



Exact active portfolio is DATA-DEPENDENT.

## 10. Technical Data & Units — LOCKED



Technical values may only come from verified laboratory reports, official technical sheets or other approved source documents.



Never invent or estimate a technical value.



Relevant fields may include density, water absorption, compressive strength, flexural strength, abrasion and other values actually present in source reports.



Test Method means the applicable standard/procedure identifier such as an EN or ASTM reference when verified.



Store one canonical technical value. Convert client-side for display.



Where applicable:

- kg/m³ ↔ lb/ft³

- MPa ↔ psi

- mm ↔ in

- °C ↔ °F



Metric/Imperial preference should persist across relevant pages/visits.



The control belongs near technical data, not in the global navigation.

## 17. Responsive Strategy — LOCKED



Desktop = Showcase Mode.

## 18. Performance — LOCKED TARGETS

- CLS ≤ 0.1

- INP ≤ 200ms

- below-fold media lazy-loaded

- maps lazy-loaded

- PDFs not preloaded

- preserve image masters and create responsive derivatives

- use modern web image formats where appropriate

- preserve marble vein detail; avoid destructive compression

- keep video masters separate from optimized web variants

- provide poster/fallback for cinematic media



### Performance Measurement Protocol



Performance targets act as release gates only when measured with a recorded protocol.



Minimum measurement scope:

Minimum test conditions:

- documented mobile test profile

- documented throttling/network conditions

- network/throttling profile

- tool

- LCP

- CLS

- major findings

Field-data absence must be stated, not fabricated.
